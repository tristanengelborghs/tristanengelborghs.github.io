Featured · Vision-language-action

A 500M VLA on LIBERO-Goal.

I turned the SmolVLM2-500M vision-language model into a robot policy. It reads two camera views and a natural-language instruction, then outputs a chunk of continuous arm actions.

LoRA adapters on the language model's attention train 4.8M of its 465M parameters. Eight learned action queries feed a small regression head, following the OpenVLA-OFT recipe inside my own benchmark harness. It is compared with a 27M ResNet-FiLM policy trained from scratch, on the same 500 initial states.

Code & full results
  1. 01

    How it was measured

    One NVIDIA L4 GPU and a pinned LIBERO commit. Each model ran 50 episodes per task on the same initial states, and the difference is tested with a paired sign-flip permutation test. Language ablations destroy the instruction at evaluation, or in training as well.

  2. 02

    What it showed

    On Goal, the instruction is nearly everything: without it both models fall to or below the 1-in-10 guess rate. The VLA gains most where the baseline struggles (pushing the plate: 72% to 100%) and loses on the two-stage drawer-and-bowl task. Pretraining bought six points for 4.6× the GPU-hours.

  3. 03

    A bug worth finding

    LIBERO spells four of the ten Goal instructions differently in its demonstration files and its evaluator, so the baseline had been training on one instruction and rolling out on another. Training now reads each instruction exactly as the evaluator does.

Single training seed. I treat these numbers as preliminary until they replicate over at least three seeds. The from-scratch baseline already beats the published from-scratch reference on Goal (Diffusion Policy, about 68%).

    Dexterous in-hand manipulation

    A Shadow Hand (24 joints, 20 actuators, tendon-coupled finger joints) reorients a cube through intermittent contact. I built the environment, domain randomisation, fingertip touch sensing and a threaded simulator (about 1,700 control steps per second on an M1 laptop). A PPO teacher trains on privileged state; a DAgger student sees only proprioception, touch and the goal.

    Clip: the first four evaluation episodes, unselected (0, 2, 0 and 5 goals). Over 200 episodes the policy averages 1.2 goals per 5 seconds, against 0.34 for random actions. The header clip shows the best 3 of those 200.

    Robot demonstrations from human video

    This pipeline films a person doing a task and turns the video into robot demonstrations in LIBERO's format. It tracks the hand, fits the palm pose with Kabsch alignment, smooths it with a 1-Euro filter that coasts through occlusions, and retargets it to end-effector actions on the robot's 20 Hz control clock.

    Clip: the pipeline on a synthetic hand with landmark noise and a scripted occlusion, so no person is filmed.

Clinical machine learning

Epilepsy surgery, measured honestly.

For people with drug-resistant epilepsy, surgery can stop seizures, but only if the region where they start is found and removed. My thesis builds machine learning around the intracranial recordings used to find it.

Master's thesis · KU Leuven · 2025–2026

Predicting epilepsy-surgery outcome from intracranial EEG

I built the machine-learning workflow for a two-centre clinical study end to end: data management, preprocessing and quality control of intracranial recordings, and model development with masked autoencoders, graph attention networks and attention-based multiple-instance learning. A desktop research application lets clinicians upload recordings, run inference and inspect predictions in interactive 3-D, with saliency maps and electrode-level biomarkers such as high-frequency oscillations, phase–amplitude coupling and connectivity.

Literature study

What the field reports, and why to read it carefully

  1. 01

    Network biomarkers matter. High-frequency oscillations, phase–amplitude coupling and connectivity track epileptogenic tissue and outcome more closely than older single features.

  2. 02

    Models trade off. SVMs and random forests stay transparent; CNNs, recurrent networks and graph networks learn from raw time–frequency and network structure.

  3. 03

    Reported numbers run high. Cohorts are small and selected, onset-zone labels are subjective, and patient-wise validation gives lower, more realistic results. Seizure freedom after surgery should be the reference.

Side project

Restoring underwater video.

As a dive instructor I film on the reef, where water scatters and absorbs light and footage turns flat and blue with depth.

Each frame gets veil (backscatter) removal in linear light, driven by a dark-channel estimate of how much light survived, then local contrast and light sharpening. A 3-D colour lookup table fitted to one AI-edited reference frame is then applied to the whole 4K clip with FFmpeg.

On held-out pixels the error to the reference fell from 0.160 to 0.055, and the output-to-input brightness ratio moves by at most 0.007 between frames, so the grade doesn't flicker.
